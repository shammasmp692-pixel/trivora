About Us

Trivora Goods Wholesalers LLC is a specialized wholesale supplier dedicated to serving the hospitality industry. We provide high-quality products tailored to the needs of hotels, resorts, serviced apartments, and guesthouses — from guest amenities and room supplies to cleaning essentials and food & beverage items.

With a strong focus on reliability, efficiency, and customer satisfaction, Trivora streamlines procurement for hospitality businesses by offering consistent product availability, competitive pricing, and fast delivery. Our team works closely with hotel procurement managers and operations teams to ensure seamless supply chain solutions.

Headquartered in Dubai, Trivora serves clients across local and international markets, helping hotels maintain excellence in guest experience through dependable wholesale supply.
